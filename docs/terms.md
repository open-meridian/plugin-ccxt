A summary of each venue's published terms, read on 2026-11-18, not legal
advice. Read each venue's terms yourself before relying on this plugin.

This plugin declares no licence. Each venue's datasets describe its terms in
words on Data sources, and your deployment's licence there is the one
enforced.
