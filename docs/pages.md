# Pages

Each page is under **Manage**, for the plugin's admins. None shows a price.

## Connection

How the plugin reaches the venue you choose at the top: the mode (Live, or
Sample data), that no key is needed, the venue's limits and what the plugin
spends of them, its calls and its last error.

## Datasets

What the plugin serves the lake from the venue you choose: each of its
datasets and whether it is licensed, the wants it heard and the ones it keeps
current, and the venue's markets mapped to your records.

## Venues

Every venue the plugin can read: On or Off, the datasets it serves, what it
does not serve and why, the markets read, its day, and where its public API
answers from.

## Tools for agents

Each page is also a read tool on your deployment's agent surface, answering
what the page shows: `read_connection`, `read_datasets` (each for a `venue`)
and `read_venues`.
